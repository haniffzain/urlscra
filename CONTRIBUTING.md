# Contributing to URLScra

URLScra is a proprietary source-available project. Contributions are **not open by default**.

## Contribution policy

- Do not submit code, patches, assets, documentation, or other modifications unless the repository owner has expressly invited or authorized the contribution.
- Submission of a pull request does not grant the contributor any right to reuse, redistribute, modify, commercialize, sublicense, or create derivative works from URLScra.
- Any authorized contribution must be the contributor's original work or material the contributor has the legal right to submit.
- By submitting an authorized contribution, the contributor grants the URLScra copyright holder a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free right to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, sublicense, and incorporate that contribution into URLScra and related versions of the project.
- The repository owner may accept, reject, modify, or remove any contribution at their sole discretion.

For permission or contribution requests, contact the repository owner through the official GitHub repository.
